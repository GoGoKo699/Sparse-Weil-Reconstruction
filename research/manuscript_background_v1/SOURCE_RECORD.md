## Access resolutions and remaining limits

- **Bézivin 2007:** the DOI, J-STAGE article/PDF paths, and Project Euclid
  article/PDF paths did not yield the primary text. Crossref and Stewart 2012
  supply verified metadata. No uninspected theorem number or finite-data bound
  from that paper is used. Hillar's corrected primary text independently
  supplies the full-sequence classification needed here.
- **Fried 1988:** publisher metadata was available, but the original chapter
  was not obtained. The theorem is encountered through the inspected and
  explicitly credited Hillar Corollary 1.4. The 1988 publication year is
  distinct from the publisher's later electronic publication dates.
- **Versioned preprints:** some explicitly versioned arXiv PDF routes failed.
  Canonical PDFs with checked version headers or versioned HTML resolved the
  relevant passages. The older Chidambaram–Keller author PDF and the earlier
  Dupuy et al. draft are not substituted for the recorded versions.
- **Publisher restrictions:** some AMS and Elsevier pages returned access
  errors. Author-hosted papers, arXiv texts, publisher metadata/search records,
  and Crossref supplied the listed facts. The erratum metadata corroboration
  is specified above. Access errors were not bypassed.
- **Algorithmic sources:** the Brent–Zimmermann author version 0.5.9 was
  inspected; it differs from arXiv version 0.5.1. A further integration fetch
  of its PDF failed, without changing the earlier successful inspection.
  Banach's 1922 metadata was found but its primary download failed. It was
  omitted from the bibliography because the elementary contraction argument
  is supplied directly in the algorithmic note.
- **Adjacent literature:** Stewart 2012/2013 were checked to distinguish
  constant-modulus runs and cyclotomic norms from finite reconstruction.
  Yoshizaki's [arXiv:2503.06194v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.06194v1)
  was screened at abstract, introduction, and main-statement level
  (Theorems 3.2 and 4.4). Those statements concern $p$-adic convergence and
  covering-space invariants; the paper was not audited in full or used as a
  reconstruction dependency.

No newly inspected statement subsumes the two local guarantees. This is a
bounded theorem comparison, not an exhaustive thesis, software, or citation-graph
search and not an exclusion of unpublished work. External author code, Sage
experiments, and Lean formalizations were not run. No author was contacted.
The specific source access gaps above do not leave a mathematical dependency
unsupported: the claims used in the background have accessible cited support
or an explicit elementary derivation.
